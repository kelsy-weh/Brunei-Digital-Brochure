Traditional Arts & Heritage

01 / TEXTILE

Tenunan Jong Sarat

Exquisite hand-woven silk adorned with precious-looking gold and silver detailing, passed through generations as a symbol of refined heritage.

02 / MUSIC

Gulingtangan

The rhythmic heart of Brunei’s traditional music, with melodic brass gongs accompanying royal occasions and celebrations.

Kampong Ayer

A remarkable settlement on stilts that embodies community, history, and Brunei’s enduring relationship with the river.

04 • A Taste of Tradition

The Royal Gastronomy

✦ ❧ ✦
01 / NATIONAL DISH

Ambuyat

Made from sago palm starch and traditionally enjoyed with bamboo chopsticks and a variety of flavorful cacah sauces.

02 / LOCAL STAPLE

Nasi Katok

Fragrant rice paired with crispy fried chicken and spicy sambal—simple, comforting, and unmistakably Bruneian.

03 / SWEET

Kuih Cincin

Flower-shaped sweet cakes made with brown palm sugar and rice flour, fried to a golden, crunchy finish.
